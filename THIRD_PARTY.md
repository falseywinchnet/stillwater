Original Stillwater code is available under the root [MIT license](LICENSE).
The separately attributed code and assets below keep their existing notices.

## Desktop Habitats / Riverscape

Author: Chase Lean. License: MIT.
Source: https://github.com/chaseleantj/desktop-habitats
Pinned commit: `66e80ea1b6bafd25324c84c72067e750c1a1f334`.

The procedural environment, plant and fish-anatomy generators are preserved verbatim
under `tools/upstream/scenes/`. `tools/upstream/PROVENANCE.json` records source hashes.
The original README and license are preserved there as well. Geometry generated by
that code is distributed in `assets/riverscape.swscene.gz`. The Metal strand-motion,
selected fish/leaf material formulas, and C++ ground-height formula are adaptations.

The full MIT notice is distributed with the application at
`assets/licenses/desktop-habitats-MIT.txt`.

This port retains geometry and the reference composition. It does not include the
Swift/WKWebView host, browser runtime, original CPU fish simulation, feeding logic,
or the complete original PBR/transmission/postprocessing implementation. The original
backboard and image-based contact-shadow planes are omitted. Native depth shadows
replace the contact-shadow planes. Our fish motion, crabs, bubbles, audio and host
are separate implementations.

## Three.js

MIT license, copyright the Three.js authors. Its vendored modules are used only
by the offline geometry translator. They do not ship in the application bundle.
The ACES tone-mapping fit in our shader follows the Three.js implementation.
The full notice is in `assets/licenses/THREE-MIT.txt` and
`tools/upstream/vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`.

## Poly Haven materials

The six diffuse/normal JPEG files under `assets/materials/` were copied without alteration from
the pinned Desktop Habitats Riverscape assets. They contain diffuse/albedo and
OpenGL normal maps for:

- Rock Boulder Dry: https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry
- Rough Wood: https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_wood
- Sand 01: https://polyhaven.com/a/sand_01

Poly Haven makes its assets available under CC0:
https://polyhaven.com/license and https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/.
The original upstream README identifies these exact texture sources.
Asset SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `assets/SHA256SUMS`.

The matching 1K ambient-occlusion maps for Rough Wood and Rock Boulder Dry were
downloaded directly from Poly Haven on 2026-09-23, also under CC0. They remain
unaltered JPEGs on disk. The loader packs their grayscale values into the unused
alpha channels of the normal-map textures. Download URLs and provider checksums
are preserved in `docs/evidence/material-sources.json`.

No imagegen imagery, textures, sprites or source objects are included.
